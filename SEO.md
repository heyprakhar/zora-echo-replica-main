## 🕉️ Complete Cultural Wedding Guide for The Opera House

### Regional Wedding Itineraries & Traditions

#### 1. Punjabi Wedding Complete Itinerary

**Pre-Wedding Ceremonies (2-3 days before)**
1. **Rokka Ceremony**: Family engagement with shagun exchange
2. **Chooda Ceremony**: Red bangles gifted by maternal uncle
3. **Vatna**: Turmeric and mustard oil application for bride's beauty
4. **Ghara Gharaoli**: Sacred water ceremony from temple
5. **Jaggo**: Night celebration with family dancing and singing

**Wedding Day Schedule**
- **Morning**: Milni ceremony with family introductions
- **Afternoon**: Anand Karaj (Sikh ceremony) or Hindu pheras
- **Evening**: Reception with bhangra performances

**Traditional Punjabi Menu at The Opera House**
- **Starters**: Malai chicken, Afghani chicken, seekh kebabs
- **Main Course**: Dal makhani, saag makki di roti, Amritsari chole-bhature, Pindi channa
- **Desserts**: Jalebi, rabri, kulfi

**Mandap Design**: Vibrant marigold decorations in yellow and orange, traditional Punjabi motifs

#### 2. Bengali Wedding Sacred Timeline

**Pre-Wedding Rituals**
1. **Gaye Holud**: Turmeric ceremony for radiance and blessings
2. **Dodhi Mangal**: Early morning rituals with prayers

**Wedding Day Ceremonies**
1. **Shubho Drishti**: Bride carried on wooden stool, seven circles around groom
2. **Saat Paak**: Seven circumambulations representing life-long union
3. **Sindoor Daan**: Vermillion application ceremony

**Traditional Bengali Feast**
- **Fish Specialties**: Mustard hilsa, fish curry preparations

**Décor Theme**: White and red color scheme with mogra flowers, traditional alpana designs
